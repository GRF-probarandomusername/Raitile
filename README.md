# Raitile


Hello and welcome to the official Raitile App Store GitHub repository!


## What is Raitile and what's our goal?

Raitile is an ecosystem of independent programs designed to simplify game development and distribution, while giving developers more freedom and control.
The programs are:
- Raitile ... (to name) a tool that simplifies transferring assets between programs.
- Raitile ... (to name) a lightweight launcher that brings everything together in one place.
- Raitile ... (to name) an open-source application store that lets developers publish and update their games, choose how much of their net revenue they want to share with Raitile (with a minimum commission of 2%), and change that percentage whenever they want.
  
**Each program can be used independently.**

  All of this is meant to let developers focus on just one thing: building their projects.


## Why "Raitile"?

When I was trying to come up with a name for Raitile, I looked out of my window. It was raining, and I saw some roof tiles.
Imagine this with me: raindrops are applications, and the gutter is the user. What do you need to bring the water to the gutter? Roof tiles!
And that's it. Just remove the "n" to make it sound cleaner, and Raitile is born!


## Why did I decide to make Raitile?

I started thinking about developers and realized that many existing app stores could do much more to support the developers whose work attracts users and generates sales.
I thought it would be great if an app store finally dared to make that kind of "mistake".


## How can I contribute to Raitile?

There are many ways to contribute to Raitile:
- **Give feedback:**
Tell us how we can improve existing features, or suggest new ideas.
- **Make a donation (later in the project):**
Help keep Raitile's servers running.
- **Star and share the project:**
Star and share the project on GitHub if you share our vision.
- **Create your own version:**
Create your own version of Raitile, following the rules of the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3).
- **Switch to Raitile (later in the project)**
Get started with Raitile products.
- **Or just appreciate our work!**
Every bit of support counts!


## Project Status and Development Philosophy

Raitile is currently in its early planning stage.
No functional product has been released yet.
We aim to prioritize meaningful updates over frequent releases. Security fixes, important optimizations, and highly requested quality-of-life improvements should take priority over minor changes that bring little noticeable value.

**The project is being developed gradually, with feedback from the community helping shape its direction.**
